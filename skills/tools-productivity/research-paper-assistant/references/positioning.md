# Stage 1 template

This is a blank scaffold, not a scientific conclusion. All fields initially unknown unless confirmed by the user.

| Field | Structure | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Target venue/type | [journal/conference], [article type] | Pending |
| Research question | In [population/system], does/how does [factor/method] affect/relate to [outcome] compared with [comparator]? | Pending |
| Minimal claim | In [scope], [observed result] supports [bounded conclusion], based on [evidence]. | Pending |
| Contribution 1: method | [actual methodological advance] compared with [closest baseline] | Pending; omit if absent |
| Contribution 2: finding | [new, reproducible finding] under [conditions] | Pending |
| Contribution 3: data/theory | [dataset/resource/theoretical contribution] with [validation and scope] | Pending; omit if absent |
| Gap | Existing work establishes [A], but leaves [B] unresolved; our design addresses [B] by [C]. | Requires source verification |
| Evidence map | [claim] -> [figure/table] -> [output/script] -> [data/design] | Pending |
| Alternative explanation | [confounding/batch effect/selection/model artifact] -> [test or limitation] | Pending |

Do not force three novel contributions if only one is defensible. Label candidate contributions as hypotheses until supported.

First round, exactly three questions:
1. What is the discipline and specific direction?
2. What research question is the existing study intended to answer? One or two sentences are enough.
3. What journal/conference is the target? If undecided, say undecided.

Subsequent rounds: at most three questions; begin with the earliest uncertain premise, then compare against the nearest verified work and examine claim-evidence failures. Article type, strongest result, design/control, and ten closest papers are later inputs, not additional first-round questions.
